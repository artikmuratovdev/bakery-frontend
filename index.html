Ilovani o'rnatish

Bek Nonlari ilovasini mobil qurilmangizga o'rnatish uchun quyidagi ko'rsatmalarga amal qiling:

Android (Google Chrome)
  1. Brauzerning yuqori o'ng burchagidagi (⋮) uch nuqta tugmasini bosing.
  2. Menyudan "Ilovani o'rnatish" yoki "Bosh ekranga qo'shish" bandini tanlang.
iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Safari pastki qismidagi Ulashish ( Share) tugmasini bosing.
  2. Pastga varaqlab "Bosh ekranga qo'shish" (Add to Home Screen) bandini bosing.
